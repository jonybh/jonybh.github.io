The Photographer

About
Jonathan

I'm Jonathan Bahour — a car photographer and visual storyteller with a genuine obsession for automobiles and the way light interacts with metal, glass, and rubber.

Every shoot is approached with a single goal: to capture the character of the car. Whether it's a pristine exotic, a slammed JDM build, or a weathered classic, I work to find the angle and the light that makes it come alive.

Based in the DMV area, I work with car enthusiasts, private owners, dealerships, and automotive brands across the East Coast and beyond.
