About
Jonathan

I'm Jonathan Bahour — a 13-year-old car photographer based in the DMV area with a serious passion for automobiles and automotive photography.

Despite my age, I approach every shoot with professionalism and a sharp eye for detail. Whether it's finding the perfect angle, chasing the right light, or capturing the personality of a build — I put everything into making your car look its best.

I'm open to photoshoots of all kinds — exotic, JDM, classic, daily drivers, you name it. If you love your car, let's shoot it.
